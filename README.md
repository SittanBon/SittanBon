### Selected work

**01 / GenAI campaign workflow** | Delivered rollout  
Built an n8n proof of concept, tested it at two AI-enabled and two comparison locations, then rolled it out across four DACH locations. Content creation time went from about four weeks to 1.5 weeks, with human approval and GDPR data minimisation built in. **[Read the case study](https://sittanbon.github.io/genai-workflow-case-study.html)**

**02 / AI Expense Intelligence** | Internal pilot and product concept  
Interviewed 30+ SME finance users, tested OCR extraction on 500+ receipts, and designed confidence-based routing: high-confidence receipts are booked automatically, uncertain ones and items that don't match the project go to a person, duplicates are rejected, and large claims always go to the budget manager. **[Read the case study](https://sittanbon.github.io/finance-ai-case-study.html)**

**03 / Bierpreis** | Live product  
A bilingual Munich beer-price map at **[bierpreis.beer](https://bierpreis.beer/)**: around 180 venues across six districts, community price reports with an admin review queue, honest price-freshness labels, and a mobile-first map and list. **[View code](https://github.com/SittanBon/muenchner-bierpreis)**

**04 / Lead-intelligence automation** | Team project in development (WBS CODING SCHOOL)  
An n8n and LLM-agent workflow for a training-consulting firm. It collects client notes from several sources (OneNote, Google Keep and similar), turns them into structured lead profiles, scores and prioritises each lead, and routes it to the responsible consultant.

**05 / Prompt Smash!** | Independent build  
An interactive guide that teaches prompt design through four journeys, with a responsible-AI review in every journey. It runs in the browser and keeps learners' drafts on their device. **[Try it](https://sittanbon.github.io/prompt-smash/)** | [View code](https://github.com/SittanBon/prompt-smash)

**06 / Mochi Pop** | Playable independent build  
A cross-platform stress-relief tapping game built with Expo and React Native. Combos, candy, XP, pet care and unlockable looks create a playful loop, and progress saves on the device. **[Play the browser preview](https://sittanbon.github.io/mochi-pop/)** | [View code](https://github.com/SittanBon/mochi-pop)

**07 / QR Event Management** | Pilot and market learning  
Piloted a QR event product and pitched it to 40 companies, 10 of which adopted it in the first quarter. Designed the licensing strategy under which five market competitors licensed it. **[Read the case study](https://sittanbon.github.io/event-engagement-case-study.html)**
